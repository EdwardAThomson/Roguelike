# Modern Rogue - Player Guide

Welcome to Modern Rogue, a browser-based roguelike game inspired by the classic Rogue. This guide will help you understand the game's features, controls, and mechanics.

## Objective

Your goal is to explore the procedurally generated dungeon, fight monsters, collect loot, and progress through the levels to become the most powerful adventurer.

## Game Controls

- Movement: Use the arrow keys or WASD to move your character around the dungeon.
- Interact: Press 'G' to pick up items on the ground, 'E' to equip items directly from the ground.
- Inventory: Press 'I' to open your inventory and manage your items.
  - Your pack is a grid. Use number keys 1-9 (or click) to select an item; the panel on the right shows its details and how it compares with what you have equipped.
  - With an item selected, press E to equip it, U to use it, or D to drop it. Shift + number equips and Alt + number uses in one step.
  - Click an equipped item in the left column to select it, then Unequip.
- Character Screen: Press 'C' to view your character stats and allocate skill points when leveling up.
- Spellbook: Press 'B' to manage your spells and bind them to the Q/R/F/V/X hotkeys.
- Belt: The HUD shows your first three potions and wands on a belt next to the spell hotbar. Press 1, 2 or 3 (or click the slot) to drink or use one without opening the inventory.
- Layout: Press 'L' to switch the HUD between the sidebar (everything beside the map) and the action bar (bars, hotbar and gear along the bottom, with the message log over the map). Options on the main menu has the same choice. Like the graphics style ('T'), it is remembered by your browser.
- The Inventory, Character, and Spellbook screens are tabs of one game menu: the game pauses while it is open, pressing a tab's key again (or Esc) closes it, and pressing another tab's key switches tabs.
- Save: Press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac) to save your run at any time (see Saving and Loading below).
- Help: Press 'H' to open the help screen with controls and information.
- Map: Press 'M' to open the map tab (the game pauses). It has two modes, switched with 1/2 or Tab:
  - Area: the section you are in, drawn from what you have explored, with gates and where they lead.
  - World: every section you have visited on the world grid (north up), with dashed outlines for neighbours you have not entered. Pan with arrows/WASD or by dragging, zoom with +/- or the mouse wheel, Space recenters on you and F fits everything you know. Click a section to open it in Area mode.
- Map Reveal: Press Shift+M to toggle between revealing the entire map and showing only explored areas (cheat mode).

## Dungeon Exploration

The game features procedurally generated dungeons with interconnected rooms. Explore the dungeon, discover new areas, and find valuable loot. The dungeon is divided into levels, and you must find the gate key to unlock the gate and progress to the next level.

Sections come in one of three themes (cave, castle, or crypt), each with its own visual style and a preference for certain monsters, so different areas both look and feel distinct. The current section's theme is shown next to its coordinates in the HUD.

## Combat

Engage in combat with monsters inhabiting the dungeon. Move your character towards a monster to initiate an attack. Your character's attributes and equipment will determine the outcome of the battle. Defeating monsters grants experience points and potentially drops loot.

## Character Progression

As you gain experience points by defeating monsters and completing objectives, your character will level up. Upon leveling up, you will receive skill points that you can allocate to improve your character's attributes:

- Strength: Increases physical damage and carrying capacity.
- Dexterity: Improves attack speed and evasion.
- Constitution: Increases maximum health and resistance to damage.
- Intelligence: Boosts magical abilities and mana pool.

## Items and Equipment

Throughout your journey, you will find various items and equipment to aid you:

- Weapons: Increase your character's damage output.
- Armor: Enhances your character's defense and resistance to damage.
- Accessories: Provide unique bonuses and effects.
- Consumables: Items that can be used for immediate benefits, such as healing potions or scrolls.

Manage your inventory wisely and equip the best items to suit your playstyle and overcome challenges.

## Saving and Loading

Press Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on Mac) to save your run at any time. The game also autosaves whenever you move between world sections. To resume a saved run, use the Continue button on the main menu.

When starting a new game via Quick Play, you choose one of two run modes:

- Adventure: death sends you back to the menu, and Continue resumes from your last save.
- Hardcore: death erases your save; the run is over for good.

The mode is fixed for the whole run.

## Tips

- Explore thoroughly to uncover hidden rooms and valuable loot.
- Prioritize upgrading your equipment to increase your combat effectiveness.
- Use consumables strategically to overcome tough encounters.
- Allocate skill points based on your preferred playstyle and character build.

Enjoy your adventure in Modern Rogue, and may fortune favor the bold!
